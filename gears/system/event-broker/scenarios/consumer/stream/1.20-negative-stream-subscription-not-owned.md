# Stream open by a principal that did not JOIN the subscription → 403 SubscriptionNotOwned

A subscription belongs to the principal that JOINed it: the subject, its tenant and its subject type, as the JOIN's credentials carried them (token scopes are not part of it, so the joiner holding another token is still the joiner). Only that principal may open its stream. Another principal, even one of the same tenant, is refused with `403 Permission Denied` (`SubscriptionNotOwned`) right after the tenant check: before the positions check and the stream lease, and with no policy call beyond the tenant check. Reading the subscription (`GET /v1/subscriptions/{sub_id}`) keeps the tenant check alone and answers `200` to that principal.

## Setup

- The deployment authenticates with static tokens. `<principal-a-token>` and `<principal-b-token>` are bound to two subjects of the same tenant `{tenant_id}`.
- Principal A creates a group `{group_id}` (`POST /v1/consumer-groups`), JOINs it with one interest on `gts.cf.core.events.topic.v1~acme.orders.v1` for `{tenant_id}` → `{sub_id}`, and SEEKs every assigned partition to `earliest`.

## Request (principal B)

```http
GET /v1/events:stream?subscription={sub_id} HTTP/1.1
Host: broker.example.com
Authorization: Bearer <principal-b-token>
Accept: multipart/mixed
```

## Expected response

- `403 Permission Denied` (`SubscriptionNotOwned`)

```json
{
  "type": "gts://gts.cf.core.errors.err.v1~cf.core.err.permission_denied.v1~",
  "title": "Permission Denied",
  "status": 403,
  "detail": "You do not have permission to perform this operation",
  "instance": "/v1/events:stream",
  "trace_id": "<request-trace-id>",
  "context": {
    "reason": "subscription '{sub_id}' was joined by another principal",
    "resource_type": "gts.cf.core.events.subscription.v1~"
  }
}
```

## Side effects

- No stream is opened for `{sub_id}`: principal A's `GET /v1/events:stream?subscription={sub_id}` then answers `200`.
