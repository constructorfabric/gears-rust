# LEAVE by a principal that did not JOIN the subscription → 403 SubscriptionNotOwned

A subscription belongs to the principal that JOINed it: the subject, its tenant and its subject type, as the JOIN's credentials carried them (token scopes are not part of it). Only that principal may LEAVE it. Another principal, even one of the same tenant, is refused with `403 Permission Denied` (`SubscriptionNotOwned`) right after the tenant check, with no policy call beyond it. A subscription its joiner abandons is reaped instead: at `join_timeout` when no stream has opened on it, at its `session_timeout` after its stream closed, or earlier by a later JOIN to its group once its stream has closed.

## Setup

- The deployment authenticates with static tokens. `<principal-a-token>` and `<principal-b-token>` are bound to two subjects of the same tenant `{tenant_id}`.
- Principal A creates a group `{group_id}` (`POST /v1/consumer-groups`) and JOINs it with one interest on `gts.cf.core.events.topic.v1~acme.orders.v1` for `{tenant_id}` → `{sub_id}`.

## Request (principal B)

```http
DELETE /v1/subscriptions/{sub_id} HTTP/1.1
Host: broker.example.com
Authorization: Bearer <principal-b-token>
```

## Expected response

- `403 Permission Denied` (`SubscriptionNotOwned`)

```json
{
  "type": "gts://gts.cf.core.errors.err.v1~cf.core.err.permission_denied.v1~",
  "title": "Permission Denied",
  "status": 403,
  "detail": "You do not have permission to perform this operation",
  "instance": "/v1/subscriptions/{sub_id}",
  "trace_id": "<request-trace-id>",
  "context": {
    "reason": "subscription '{sub_id}' was joined by another principal",
    "resource_type": "gts.cf.core.events.subscription.v1~"
  }
}
```

## Side effects

- `{sub_id}` stays a member of `{group_id}`: principal A's `GET /v1/subscriptions/{sub_id}` then answers `200`.
