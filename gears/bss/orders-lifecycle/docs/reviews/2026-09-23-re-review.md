## HIGH
- **RR-H1 [B] `sales_path` proxy can be wrong in the unsafe direction.** `sales_path` is fixed at
  create, but delegation can change before submit: (a) partner creates in its own tenant without a
  proof (`self_service`), PATCHes `resourceTenantId` (editable in draft, `02:601`) to a customer and
  submits with a proof; (b) a buyer creates, a delegated partner submits (`08:1025`). Both stay
  `self_service`, so the recording-party bar is skipped and the self-service automatic acceptance at
  submit (`08:1170`, 05 §3.6/§4.2) can record the partner's submit as the customer's consent —
  what D-31/D-130 prevent. `01:1279` "imprecise in one direction … applies more, never less" is false.
  Fix: key the submit-time acceptance on facts of the submit request (no proof on the allowed
  submit and submitter tenant = resource tenant), or recompute/freeze `sales_path` at submit.

## MEDIUM

  Fix: closed token per trigger; move expiry details into the contribution / `data`.
