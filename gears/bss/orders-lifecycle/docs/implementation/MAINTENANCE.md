# S2-11 maintenance infrastructure, retention and audit verification

Status: **implemented, independently gap-fix reviewed and locally verified** ([implementation report](../../../../../artifacts/orders-lifecycle-s2-20261006/S2-11-implementation.md), [gap review](../../../../../artifacts/orders-lifecycle-s2-20261006/S2-11-gap-review.md)). The gap review (2026-10-07) fixed the audit namespace roster (D-209 item 6), added the cleanup keyset cursor and recovery-first ordering (item 7), the checkpoint mismatch withholding and honest checkpoint metrics (item 8), and recorded the retention `SKIP LOCKED` substitution and lock release on every exit path (item 9).

- **Scope:** the [five-worker roster and coordination contract](../DESIGN.md#contract-01-3-8), the [retention purge](../features/01-foundation.md#35-purge-bounded-retention-rows) (D-185), the [idempotency cleanup](../features/01-foundation.md#31-resolve-idempotency-ownership-and-retention) item 6 with the D-188/D-198 recovery continuation port, and the [audit verifier and D-100 checkpoints](../DESIGN.md#contract-01-audit).
- **Decisions:** [D-209](../DECISIONS.md#d-209--worker-class-connections-the-conditional-sweep-lock-and-streamed-checkpoint-publication-s2-11).

The package delivers the generic recovery and maintenance infrastructure (the shared-ownership row of the [implementation README](README.md)). The expiry and draft auto-void **bodies** (TTL policy evaluation and the engine's `transition_internal` entry) are Stage 5 deliveries; the D-188 attempt and D-198 control **recovery bodies** are S3/S5 deliveries. Each slot is scheduled, locked, connected and measured here and reports `unavailable` until its body arrives: no candidate is read, transitioned, abandoned or purged by an undelivered body.

| Audit worker | [infra/workers/audit.rs](../../orders-lifecycle/src/infra/workers/audit.rs). The roster of namespaces is the union of live orders, checkpoint headers and committed rows (`scope::discover_audit_namespaces`, D-209 item 6), so a namespace whose trail or only order was removed stays scheduled. Per namespace under `audit/<uuid>`: a **checkpoint phase** on the checkpoint connection (one `REPEATABLE READ` snapshot; previous header, live inventory, counters and heads from that snapshot; members streamed in ascending binary `order_id` order into the transaction through `CheckpointDigest`; counter/head, overflow, prior-prefix and vanished-order reconciliation; header written last; `(audit_tenant_id, checkpoint_sequence)` uniqueness rejects a competing append and rolls every member back; after a mismatch the next capture is withheld for one interval while the verifier keeps alerting and the checkpoint age keeps growing, D-209 item 8) and a **verification phase** on the verifier connection (read-only repeatable-read slices: checkpoint history recomputed and chained, every order's committed chain recomputed with `ChainVerifier`, counter reconciled, every recorded member compared with the recomputed chain). ## Limitations and hand-offs

1. **Bodies not delivered here.** Expiry/auto-void (Stage 5: TTL policy, `transition_internal`), D-188 attempt recovery (S3) and D-198 control recovery (S5) plug into `TransitionSweep` and `ExecutionRecovery`; until then the slots report `unavailable` and lapsed unresolved executions are preserved and counted, never abandoned.
2. **Capacity is a local sample.** The 520-order sample (one checkpoint ≈ 0.15 s, full verification ≈ 0.23 s on the test container) demonstrates bounded paging, not production capacity; the pre-production demonstration at the D-41 baseline remains a release gate.
3. **Verification coverage is per replica.** The rolling cursor and coverage age live in the replica that holds the namespace key; a replica restart restarts its pass, and the mismatch capture hold is forgotten with it. With several replicas contending for one namespace key, each replica advances only on the ticks it wins, so a full pass can take up to the replica count times longer than the single-replica figure and the per-replica coverage-age gauge can cross 30 days without the namespace being unverified by every replica. DESIGN forbids a sixth worker and a fence table; persisting the cursor would be a new schema object and needs a DESIGN decision ([gap review](../../../../../artifacts/orders-lifecycle-s2-20261006/S2-11-gap-review.md), open question 1). The cleanup cursor is replica-local in the same way and only revisits.
4. **External anchoring (S6-07)** is not configured; checkpoint export/anchoring is outside this package.
5. **Deployment provisioning.** `LOGIN` members of the five group roles, their DSN secrets and the attested direct/session-pooled lock route are deployment work (S2-12 for the E2E host; S6 for production).
6. **Pre-capture loss is not evidenced**, as D-100 item 5 states and the tamper test demonstrates: an order removed with its counter before its first checkpoint produces no finding.
7. **Attestation probes cover Orders tables only.** The retention class holds no grant on the platform outbox tables (migration 08 grants them to the runtime role alone), but the startup census has no negative probe on `toolkit_outbox_*`; that absence is proved by the migration-08 grant inventory tests, not by the census.
8. **Tiered-history verification** (the D-100 acceptance item for archived tiers) has no archive tier to run against yet; verification reads the single live store.
