### R-01 — Usage feed

- **Current state**: code (this branch and upstream `main`): `UsageCollectorClientV1` offers
  `list_usage_records` (OData keyset page over `(created_at, id)`, not an acceptance-order cursor)
  and `deactivate_usage_record` (in-place `status` flip, cascading to `corrects_id` compensations);
  records are instants. Documents (upstream `main`, commit `5de85f067`, 15 ADRs): a breaking pre-1.0
  V1 with `read_usage_feed(FeedSubscription, FeedStart {Oldest, After}, until, limit)`, interval
  entries with `entry_type`, invalidation as an entry, a six-part dedup identity behind the entry id,
  a backfill route, watermarks only on an operator reconciliation route, and type declarations in
  types-registry. The collector's DESIGN calls the code known debt and itself normative
  (DESIGN:2296). Since the rebase onto `main` (2026-10-05) this branch carries these documents
  (`5de85f067` is an ancestor of the branch head); the earlier note that its base `8aca4d6df` carried
  the older documents is no longer true.
- **Problem**: polling by event time misses late-accepted records behind the cursor and cannot see
  invalidations; a status flip is invisible mutation of financial input.
- **Options**: (a) usage-collector implements its documented V1 feed; (b) Rating polls
  `list_usage_records` by `created_at` with overlap windows; (c) the Atlas `UsageFeedV1` snapshot
  feed.
- **Recommendation**: (a), which the Atlas Plus overlay also asks for ("withdraw UsageFeedV1 and
  regenerate C05 from the Collector's design"). (b) is unsafe because `created_at` is
  caller-supplied and not an acceptance sequence, and a status flip is invisible mutation of financial
  input; (c) has no owner.

### R-02 — Pricing read contract

- **Current state on `main`**: `PricingReadV1::resolve` (`GET /bss-pricing/v1/resolve`) answers a
  published or superseded plan revision on one date with the whole chain matrix per item; `price`
  (`GET /bss-pricing/v1/prices/{id}`) serves an approved price's money forever; `current_revision`
  names the revision in force. Golden contracts freeze both on SQLite and Postgres (T-D-37
  amendment). `pin_frontier` and the `CatalogVersion` read model are legacy; pricing refuses a
  legacy schema (`m0000_pricing_refuse_a_legacy_or_stale_schema.rs`).
- **Consequence**: the blocker is gone; the work moves to Rating's adapter (R-17).

### R-03 — Subscriptions contract

- **Current state**: design prose; `BillableItemCreated` designed as outbox CloudEvents with
  "sufficiency, not schema"; no attribution, no recovery reads.
- **Recommendation**: the Atlas C04 surface — `BillableFactPublished` (versioned facts),
  `BillableSetSealed`, `AttributionSegmentChanged`, `UsageScopeSealed`, and
  `SubscriptionBillingReadV1::{facts, period_facts, segments_since, scope_for_window, bindings}`
  for recovery. Rating consumes events **and** reads through one inbox (T-D-56), so it works with or
  without a broker.

### R-04 — Period state owner (revised 2026-10-01)

- **Current state**: no Billing gear; no producer of `periodState`; the ledger's fiscal period is a
  different concept with no read API.
- **Previous recommendation (2026-09-25, T-D-47)**: a Rating-owned `open → closed` fence closed by
  Billing's `close_period`, with signed delta entries routed `post_close`.
- **Revised recommendation**: Atlas D09. Rating publishes the complete exact result of every parent
  fact at a monotonic `result_revision`; Billing compares each revision with its latest accepted
  target (including pending postings) and alone decides between replacing a draft and issuing a
  credit/debit note. `BillingPeriodStateChanged` is an observational hint and never gates Rating.
- **Trade-offs**: removes the cross-gear fence and the period row lock from Rating's hot path and
  makes rounding residues impossible to double-count (Billing compares rounded aggregates, F14/F32);
  requires Billing to keep per-fact accepted heads (Atlas C08).

### R-06 — Level meters

- **Options**: (a) amend the UC PRD so a charging consumer may integrate `MAX`/`LATEST` series and
  keep T-D-17; (b) adopt UC's rule — level products are emitted as `SUM` meters in the billable unit
  (GB·h per hour; hourly peak as a distinct `SUM` meter, one record per hour).
- **Recommendation**: (b), which the Atlas also takes (R1, F-A9). Interim: a child whose
  derived usage declaration has a `Peak` or `TimeWeighted` input fails closed
  (`unsupported_input_fold`, T-D-76); the record itself is still counted. Needs Product, Pricing and
  Usage Collector sign-off because T-D-17 was a confirmed product call.

### R-07 — FX

- **Recommendation**: launch native-currency only (DESIGN §2.2); add FX when the ledger or a Finance
  gear exposes pinnable rate snapshots (J-8).

### R-11 — Contracts / Promotions inputs

- **Recommendation**: the dormant adjustment slots of step 4 (contract override, commitments and
  reservations, coupons) run with empty inputs; a binding referencing a promotion or a
  reservation needing a contract match fails closed (`coupon_source_unavailable`,
  `reservation_match_unavailable`). `CommitmentBalanceEffect` publication (T-D-10/T-D-27) is not
  built until a Contracts consumer exists.

### R-17 — Pricing adapter against the PriceBook read contract

Closed 2026-10-08. The adapter is T-D-73 (bindings and the binding of record), T-D-74 (money),
T-D-75 (windows and aggregation), T-D-76 (derived meters), T-D-77 (proration) and T-D-78 (the
minimum-fee floor); T-D-79 aligns order evaluation with Orders. What still blocks live rating is
upstream and is listed in UPSTREAM_REQS: Subscriptions carrying the period's pins, plan revision,
quantity and `BillingTerms` on each fact; the Products derived-declaration read (R-31).

### R-21 — Finalization evidence

Target (Atlas C05/C10, T-D-52): a child window is final when `now ≥ window.to + delay` **and** its
ownership scope is sealed, the attribution projection has reached the scope's sequence, every
expected resource interval has a final coverage declaration, and the record count, quantity sum and
record-set digest Rating computes over its stored entries for those resources match the
declarations. The collector never judges completeness and has no snapshot token (its ADR-0011); the
emitter that would declare coverage has no owner (overlay T6: IRM excludes metering, the collector
never judges completeness). Without those sources none of the evidence can be proven. A `delay_only` profile is an explicit, recorded business acceptance
that delay elapsed is treated as the finalization trigger for that profile; it is never the default,
and it never overrides a known source loss: an open loss blocks finality under `delay_only` exactly
as under `full` (T-D-62).

**Pricing D-372 amendment (2026-09-16), T-D-35:** row selection uses `(skuId, dimensionKey)`. The SKU is the priced row's registry resource identity; `meter` is derived and frozen for measurement-model mapping, and two resource SKUs may share it. **Pricing D-419–D-422, D-424 and D-425 amendment (2026-09-26), T-D-37/T-D-38:** the read contract Rating adapts against is built and frozen. `GET /bss-pricing/v1/resolve?plan_revision_id=&date=&item_id=&pins=` (D-419) answers a `published` or `superseded` revision on one date, the period start; `item_id` narrows the answer to one item; `pins` are the subscription's current bindings, comma-separated, each `<price_id>` (the price a chain is bound to) or `<price_id>:<dim_value>` (a default-chain price a value was bound to), at most 1 000 per request. Refusals: 400 `QUERY_INVALID`, `DATE_INVALID`, `PIN_FOREIGN` (a pin that does not parse, that is not an approved price of the tenant on an entry the revision names, or that pins a value to a price outside the default chain refuses the whole request), `PIN_DUPLICATE`, `PINS_TOO_MANY`; 404 for an unknown or another tenant's revision and for an item the revision lacks; 409 `REVISION_NOT_PUBLISHED`; Products' own refusal as Products gave it; 503 `REGISTRY_UNAVAILABLE`. Per item the answer is the whole matrix (D-420) — the default chain, every value registered today and any value a pin names — and per chain either a binding or `uncovered: true`, never a refusal and never an invented price: a signup binds the price in force (the value's own chain, else the default; `dim_used` says which); a renewal walks the pinned chain through `all` successors and stops before the first `new` one (`pinned_from` names the pin); a binding is always a price in force, so a walk that ends on an ended price binds as a signup would; a value bound to the default moves to its own later `all` price. A binding carries `price_id`, `dim_used`, `pinned_from`, `model`, `price` (the model's money object as stored: `{amount}` for flat; `{rate}` for per_unit; `{tiers: [{up_to, rate}]}` for graduated and volume, the last band's `up_to` null; `{package_size, package_price}` for package — every amount exact decimal text), `min_fee`, `eligibility`, `effective_from`, `effective_to`, `temporary_until`, `ends_on` and `keep_for_bound`; the item carries `sku_version` as of the date (`published_version`, `effective_from`; null when Products has no version then or does not know the SKU), `meter` (`usage_type_ref`, `unit`) and the invoice inputs `invoice_line_template`, `gl_code`, `tax_category` and `billing_timing`, each `{ value, source }` with source `entry`, `sku`, `tenant` or null (D-421); the revision carries `currency`, `currency_minor_digits` and `rounding_policy`. Rating slices a period at a binding's `ends_on`, the price's own end (a temporary price's end or an explicit close), never at `effective_to`, the stored window, which the start of a `new` successor the pin did not take also sets (pricing D-425). At an `ends_on` inside a period Rating resolves again with the pin on that date for the rest of the period. No totals and no promotion (pricing D-409, D-415). A replay reads the pinned money at `GET /bss-pricing/v1/prices/{id}` (D-422), served forever for an approved price whatever its window; any other id is one and the same 404. T-D-38 stands as decided: the minimum-fee floor and proration are Rating's arithmetic over `min_fee` and the slices between the period start, each `ends_on` and the period end, and pricing computes neither. The legacy joint fixture corpus, its proration family included, was deleted with `gears/bss/fixtures/` in pricing phase 4, so Rating's proration and floor cases are Rating's to author.

**Pricing D-467 amendment (2026-09-30), T-D-38:** a pricing plan item is now a SKU and its entry in the plan's book (the pricing owner, 2026-09-30).
