**Pricing D-372 amendment (2026-09-16), T-D-35:** row selection uses `(skuId, dimensionKey)`. The SKU is the priced row's registry resource identity; `meter` is derived and frozen for measurement-model mapping, and two resource SKUs may share it. The plan-level SKU continues to name the sold offer.

**Pricing D-419–D-422, D-424 and D-425 amendment (2026-09-26), T-D-37/T-D-38:** the read contract Rating adapts against is built and frozen. `GET /bss-pricing/v1/resolve?plan_revision_id=&date=&item_id=&pins=` (D-419) answers a `published` or `superseded` revision on one date, the period start; `item_id` narrows the answer to one item; `pins` are the subscription's current bindings, comma-separated, each `<price_id>` (the price a chain is bound to) or `<price_id>:<dim_value>` (a default-chain price a value was bound to), at most 1 000 per request. Refusals: 400 `QUERY_INVALID`, `DATE_INVALID`, `PIN_FOREIGN` (a pin that does not parse, that is not an approved price of the tenant on an entry the revision names, or that pins a value to a price outside the default chain refuses the whole request), `PIN_DUPLICATE`, `PINS_TOO_MANY`; 404 for an unknown or another tenant's revision and for an item the revision lacks; 409 `REVISION_NOT_PUBLISHED`; Products' own refusal as Products gave it; 503 `REGISTRY_UNAVAILABLE`. Per item the answer is the whole matrix (D-420) — the default chain, every value registered today and any value a pin names — and per chain either a binding or `uncovered: true`, never a refusal and never an invented price: a signup binds the price in force (the value's own chain, else the default; `dim_used` says which); a renewal walks the pinned chain through `all` successors and stops before the first `new` one (`pinned_from` names the pin); a binding is always a price in force, so a walk that ends on an ended price binds as a signup would; a value bound to the default moves to its own later `all` price. A binding carries `price_id`, `dim_used`, `pinned_from`, `model`, `price` (the model's money object as stored: `{amount}` for flat; `{rate}` for per_unit; `{tiers: [{up_to, rate}]}` for graduated and volume, the last band's `up_to` null; `{package_size, package_price}` for package — every amount exact decimal text), `min_fee`, `eligibility`, `effective_from`, `effective_to`, `temporary_until`, `ends_on` and `keep_for_bound`; the item carries `sku_version` as of the date (`published_version`, `effective_from`; null when Products has no version then or does not know the SKU), `meter` (`usage_type_ref`, `unit`) and the invoice inputs `invoice_line_template`, `gl_code`, `tax_category` and `billing_timing`, each `{ value, source }` with source `entry`, `sku`, `tenant` or null (D-421); the revision carries `currency`, `currency_minor_digits` and `rounding_policy`. Rating slices a period at a binding's `ends_on`, the price's own end (a temporary price's end or an explicit close), never at `effective_to`, the stored window, which the start of a `new` successor the pin did not take also sets (pricing D-425). At an `ends_on` inside a period Rating resolves again with the pin on that date for the rest of the period. No totals and no promotion (pricing D-409, D-415). A replay reads the pinned money at `GET /bss-pricing/v1/prices/{id}` (D-422), served forever for an approved price whatever its window; any other id is one and the same 404. The frozen answers are pricing's golden consumer contracts, `gears/bss/pricing/pricing/tests/contract/*.json` — fourteen files, the same answers on SQLite and Postgres — and Rating's adapter tests read them. A Rating caller, a system subject, needs only pricing `plan:read` (resolve) and `price:read` (the pinned read): resolve reads each SKU version as pricing's system actor (pricing D-424). T-D-38 stands as decided: the minimum-fee floor and proration are Rating's arithmetic over `min_fee` and the slices between the period start, each `ends_on` and the period end, and pricing computes neither. The legacy joint fixture corpus, its proration family included, was deleted with `gears/bss/fixtures/` in pricing phase 4, so Rating's proration and floor cases are Rating's to author.

**Pricing D-467 amendment (2026-09-30), T-D-38:** a pricing plan item is now a SKU and its entry in the plan's book (the pricing owner, 2026-09-30). No plan item is optional or included, and none carries an included or a minimum quantity: `GET /bss-pricing/v1/resolve` answers no `treatment`, `included_qty` or `qty_min` on an item, and the consumer goldens were recorded again without them. So T-D-38's step "after included quantities" has no operand: Rating aggregates every value and slice rated by one price and applies that price's prorated floor before promotions, with no included quantity to deduct first. An item stored before D-467 without an entry still resolves, with `price_book_entry_id: null` and no chains, as an item without an entry always did.
