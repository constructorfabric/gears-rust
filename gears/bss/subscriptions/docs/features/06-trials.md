**Status:** target design for optional PlanRevision free-period or monetary-credit trials and shared credit accounting. S-U12 schema, S-U15 provider/Ledger integration, bounded financial coverage/enforcement and executed recovery evidence remain release gates.

**Credit-trial activation contract.** Billing commits one reserved promotional grant to the accepted acquisition and exact activation operation before Entitlements prepares access. Its amount, currency, scope and duration are fixed. The commitment guarantees coverage from successful Subscriptions activation T. Entitlements establishes usable readiness under that commitment, with authorized pre-T preparation borne by the platform and no pre-T bonus drawdown. Subscriptions records T, episode, commitment and publication intent atomically.

Billing then records the same effective grant without another issuance decision. Delayed recording does not gate initial access, shift T/expiry or create another grant. Only a proved non-activation fence permits release. Unknown outcomes retain the commitment. Current cancellation, expiry and exhaustion still govern eligibility. Exact SDK/provider mappings, bounded exposure/enforcement and executed recovery evidence remain delivery gates.

## 1. Feature Context

### 1.1 Overview

Apply the accepted optional free-period OR monetary-credit trial block of Pricing PlanRevision. Use ordinary subscription states and composition boundaries. Preserve trial evidence and require the agreed settlement arrangement before paid-target conversion.

### 1.2 Purpose

**Requirements:** `cpt-cf-bss-subscriptions-fr-trials-not-a-status`, `cpt-cf-bss-subscriptions-fr-trial-commercial-pattern`, `cpt-cf-bss-subscriptions-fr-trial-provisioning`, `cpt-cf-bss-subscriptions-fr-trial-conversion`, `cpt-cf-bss-subscriptions-fr-trial-early-conversion`, `cpt-cf-bss-subscriptions-fr-trial-expiry`.

**Principle:** `cpt-cf-bss-subscriptions-principle-manifest-closed-enum`.

### 1.3 Actors

| Actor | Role |
| --- | --- |
| `cpt-cf-bss-subscriptions-actor-customer` | Accepts the trial/paid target, requests early conversion or cancellation. |
| `cpt-cf-bss-subscriptions-actor-product-manager` | Approves commercial trial policy authored in Pricing; Products supplies SKU identity/structure, while Contracts contributes only explicitly bound terms. |
| `cpt-cf-bss-subscriptions-actor-platform-operator` | Inspects and recovers existing trial operations; cannot extend or repeat a trial. |

### 1.4 References

- **PRD:** [PRD.md](../PRD.md); **Design:** [capability gates](../DESIGN.md#41-capability-gates-and-source-precedence).
- **Build prerequisites:** [foundation](01-foundation.md), [composition](02-composition.md), [renewal/retention](04-renewal-retention.md) and [entitlement posture](05-entitlements.md), as allocated in [DECOMPOSITION.md](../DECOMPOSITION.md).
- **Integration acceptance:** trial boundary/fact conformance with [commercial facts](08-commercial-facts.md) must pass before its billing effects are enabled; it is not an additional trial-feature build dependency. External trial/payment/Entitlements contracts remain the launch gates below.
- **Current trial policy:** extension and repeat trials are deferred, including with explicit Pricing permission. Preserve payer/plan history and refuse repeat acquisitions across subscription IDs; retry recovers the original episode. No additional bonus, stacking, rollover or reactivation. Early valid conversion preserves eligible unused credit under its original expiry and restrictions.
- **Required concrete parameters and owner contracts:** explicit trial identity, eligible products/duration, trial/paid terms, authoritative settlement evidence, retry/later paid association, detailed postpay due/dunning, compatible acceptance/hold handling and Entitlements target/expiry protocol under S-Q06. Settlement-scheme and prepay-failure default directions are accepted; accepted trial/paid-target start restrictions and original grant expiry remain binding. Applicable later paid routing additionally requires S-U01 association/recovery agreement.

4. [ ] - `p1` - Record attributable trial service/usage under the accepted policy. ### Scheduled or early conversion

- [ ] `p1` - **ID**: `cpt-cf-bss-subscriptions-flow-trial-conversion-trl`

**Steps**:
1. [ ] - `p1` - Authorize and replay the operation, then acquire the trial-episode and aggregate fences. An already converted/expired episode cannot convert again under another transport key. - `inst-authorize-replay-operation-acquire`
2. [ ] - `p1` - Check pending cancellation/opt-out at or before conversion first. A pending or failed unresolved cancellation continues suppressing paid conversion. - `inst-check-pending-cancellation-opt`
3. [ ] - `p1` - If suspended, defer the scheduled conversion without advancing terms or paid posture. The trial episode still ends at its original expiry; suspension does not extend it. Before the original expiry, guarded resume may remove the separate suspension while retaining any pending-conversion restriction. The parked conversion may then commit as its own guarded operation at actual `T_paid`; resume itself does not apply it. At or after expiry, close the parked conversion without application and keep the episode expired. Paid service then requires a separately authorized Orders acquisition; resume cannot use the closed trial as its purchase authority or automatically reuse resources. Resume never restores trial access or credit after the original expiry. Terminal cancellation voids the parked conversion. - `inst-suspended-defer-scheduled-conversion`
4. [ ] - `p1` - Validate the already accepted paid target under the versioned Pricing trial contract and obtain current operation authority plus the applicable FA financial permission. Unchanged terms need no new customer assent merely for conversion/retry; changed or previously unaccepted terms require explicit assent through a supported route. An expired initial V1 hold is not paid-target authority. Prepay requires payment prescribed by agreed terms before the paid transition; a stored method alone is insufficient. - `inst-validate-already-accepted-paid`
   1. [ ] - `p1` - Postpay requires accepted postpay terms and authoritative authorization to serve on credit, without conversion-time payment or a linked card. No universal capture mechanism is inferred. Relevant unknown/outage is not absence or permission; unresolved required evidence cannot authorize conversion or extend the trial, while invoice/postpay does not acquire an unrelated card-provider dependency. - `inst-postpay-requires-accepted-postpay`
5. [ ] - `p1` - Scheduled prepay collection starts at `E − conversionLeadTime` from the accepted trial terms and must reach a definitive outcome before E (SUB-D-65); an outcome still unknown at E is handled as normal expiry, and a capture that succeeds after E is remediated by refund or, with explicit customer assent, a new purchase, never by reviving the episode. If no applicable authorized settlement route exists at expiry, use normal trial expiry/revocation, with no automatic successor paid-target period/posture/facts, debt or setup charge caused by expiry; legitimate trial facts remain available for rating and bonus allocation. An authorized postpay route instead permits conversion and paid accrual from each component's `B` under SUB-D-67; invoice/payment follow its agreed due date. Delinquency applies only once due and unpaid, then follows agreed recorded failure/retry/restriction policy. - `inst-scheduled-prepay-collection-starts`
   1. [ ] - `p1` - Definitive prescribed-prepay failure at trial end records failure and ends the episode with no paid start; normal expiry/revocation retains data. A collection retry may support this conversion only while the episode remains eligible and the paid commit occurs before original E. Payment after closure follows refund or, with explicit customer assent, a new paid Orders purchase; it cannot restart this conversion. Trial extension is deferred; no automatic grace/free extension or paid-renewal grace is inherited. Stage the commercial Entitlements posture under the agreed asynchronous protocol; no synchronous materialization prerequisite is added. - `inst-definitive-prescribed-prepay-failure`
6. [ ] - `p1` - Once the prescribed payment or authorized postpay prerequisites and normal guards succeed, commit one eligible transition to accepted paid terms with actual boundary, composition revision, commercial posture and frozen fact versions. Payment success alone proves neither application nor instant Entitlements access. A successful payment permits this conversion only if its guarded paid transition commits before original E while the episode is still eligible. Component accrual starts from `B` under SUB-D-67; the waiting gap creates no retroactive coverage/debt. Payment after episode closure follows the financial remedy or separately authorized new paid Orders purchase. An expired/cancelled episode cannot use this conversion path again. Later paid acquisition requires supported guarded routing as below. - `inst-prescribed-payment-authorized-postpay`
   1. [ ] - `p1` - For early valid guarded conversion, retain unused credit on the same grant for eligible paid expenses until original expiry under unchanged currency/product/item/charge-kind restrictions. Closing the trial by this conversion does not revoke the grant; no new grant, top-up or duration is created. The bonus does not satisfy prescribed upfront payment unless the agreed coverage/settlement contract permits it. - `inst-early-valid-guarded-conversion`
7. [ ] - `p1` - Preserve enclosing commercial period identity unless the accepted model explicitly creates a new period. Publish corrected served slices to Rating. S-Q03/S-Q04 select Rating valuation and identity per agreed chargeable action; trial conversion alone is not a fee trigger. Conversion-related one-time capability remains disabled until its expressly agreed charge terms, occurrence schema and S-U08/S-U13 contracts/fixtures exist; these approvals do not expand the trial profile. - `inst-preserve-enclosing-commercial-period`
8. [ ] - `p1` - **Trial collection ownership — proposed S-U07 contract.** Subscriptions owns the scheduled `E − conversionLeadTime` trigger and the exhaustion-triggered conversion decision. It persists one collection request in the outbox for the stable conversion occurrence, bound to the trial episode, accepted terms, original E, seller/payer scope and selected financial owner. That owner obtains the authoritative obligation from Billing and directs Payments; Subscriptions does not calculate the amount or execute capture. Admission checks, disposition reads and settlement notifications are not implicit collection instructions. Scheduled and exhaustion triggers recover the same occurrence rather than creating another collection. - `inst-trial-collection-ownership-proposed`
9. [ ] - `p1` - The financial owner must fence capture start against original E and episode closure, including delayed outbox delivery and retries. No new capture may start at or after E or for a closed occurrence. Closure requests cancellation or void of unused authorization through the owner; an in-flight or unknown capture remains recoverable, and late success follows the existing refund or explicitly assented new-purchase remedy. A local timer alone cannot prove that a remote capture was stopped. The exact request/recovery and cancellation-fence protocol is an S-U07 owner requirement, not an existing SDK method. The affected prepay-conversion collection profile remains disabled until the owners agree the contract and demonstrate the race cases; postpay conversion has no collection requirement. - `inst-financial-owner-fence-capture`
10. [ ] - `p1` - Unknown payment remains recoverable under the financial owner contract, not definitive failure or success. Reconcile the original outcome before a retry can collect again; payment uncertainty cannot release protected prepaid rights, authorize conversion or extend trial access automatically. Existing paid-order remedy and pre-capture protections remain in force. - `inst-unknown-payment-remains-recoverable`

### Extend or expire

- [ ] `p2` - **ID**: `cpt-cf-bss-subscriptions-flow-trial-extension-expiry`

**Steps**:
1. [ ] - `p2` - Refuse extension and repeat-trial requests before effects even with Pricing policy or operator approval. Retain history and original expiry; create no successor trial interval or new bonus. - `inst-refuse-extension-repeat-trial`
2. [ ] - `p2` - For an active unconverted trial, duration expiry follows the accepted end action. Authoritative credit exhaustion follows `exhaustionAction` (SUB-D-66). With `convert` and satisfied prepay payment or authorized postpay service, commit the guarded paid conversion once. With `convert` and an accepted prepay route but payment still pending, atomically enter `conversion-pending`, record its effective start and reason, advance the aggregate revision, persist the restricted desired posture and commercial interval evidence, and enqueue trial-access revocation with the operation outcome. Start or recover one collection for this episode. Keep the episode eligible for paid conversion only before its original expiry; this waiting state grants no trial or paid access and creates no customer debt. Preserve data and occupancy under the normal lifecycle rules. - `inst-active-unconverted-trial-duration`
   1. [ ] - `p2` - Payment may enable conversion only if the episode is still pending, the actual commit is before original expiry, and all guards pass. A definitive payment failure, expiry or cancellation closes the pending conversion and runs the normal end action. Unknown payment remains recoverable but cannot delay expiry. Payment arriving after closure follows refund or separately authorized new-purchase recovery; it cannot reopen the episode. With `end`, or no accepted paid route, run the normal end action immediately. - `inst-payment-enable-conversion-episode`
   2. [ ] - `p2` - Revocation intent and completion are separately observable. Exhaustion grants no permission to continue service at platform cost. Actual stopping-delay usage remains platform-borne under the existing liability rule; it creates no customer debt or new credit. The hourly allocation rule in SUB-D-67 also grants no access permission. Expiry alone creates no paid period or setup occurrence. Service between original expiry and the revocation that Entitlements confirms is platform-borne, consumes no trial credit and is not a trial expense for Rating or Billing. Legitimate prior obligations and prepaid-order remedies survive. - `inst-revocation-intent-completion-separately`
3. [ ] - `p2` - Later new paid acquisition uses Orders and its normal guards; it cannot revive a terminal subscription or automatically reuse resources. Early conversion of the existing eligible trial remains in scope through its own flow. - `inst-later-new-paid-acquisition`
4. [ ] - `p2` - Consume Billing's authoritative versioned grant expiry/exhaustion outcomes under the proposed S-U15 contract. For an active unconverted credit trial, serialize the resulting trial lifecycle intent against the episode. After early conversion has already applied, retained-grant expiry/exhaustion ends only bonus coverage; it does not itself stop or revoke paid service, duplicate conversion or reverse it. Remaining paid expenses follow the component-boundary rule in SUB-D-67: the hourly remainder before `B` stays platform-borne after coverage ends, while residual from `B` follows paid terms. No payment prerequisite is waived. - `inst-consume-billing-authoritative-versioned`
5. [ ] - `p2` - One shared grant covers eligible items once; a grant ID is not a setup occurrence. Balance observations are not access enforcement or paid permission. Stale/unknown outcomes cannot create debt, authorize spending, refill a grant or revive closed access. Billing applies canonical full Rating replacements to the same grant exactly once with original terms/result/invoice lineage; a still-valid/applicable grant may regain availability from a downward correction for an active trial or eligible paid usage after early guarded conversion, until the same original expiry. - `inst-shared-grant-covers-eligible`
6. [ ] - `p2` - After grant expiry, Billing corrects history/accounting while spendable credit stays zero and original expiration stays unchanged; correction cannot extend the trial, reopen the episode or repeat conversion. A later correction cannot automatically reopen a trial already ended by exhaustion, even before original expiry: restored accounting balance grants no service/spend permission for that closed episode and does not reverse an applied paid conversion. V1 has no reactivation route; a new acquisition is a fresh paid purchase; repeat trials are deferred. - `inst-grant-expiry-billing-corrects`
7. [ ] - `p2` - For eligible expenses before the component's paid rating/allocation boundary `B`, the platform bears full rated value beyond bounded, applicable bonus coverage, including positive corrections and in-flight overrun; it creates no customer trial-overage debt. `B` is the actual paid transition `T_paid` for non-hourly components and the next UTC-hour boundary at or after `T_paid` for hourly components. The hourly remainder `[T_paid, B)` therefore retains platform liability after bonus coverage. From `B`, residual follows accepted paid-plan/settlement terms. Original grant expiry `E` remains exclusive: no service at or after `E` spends trial credit. Without paid conversion, stopping-delay usage remains platform-borne and creates no paid boundary. Classify by served evidence and these frozen boundaries, never arrival time or guessed time proration (SUB-D-67). - `inst-eligible-expenses-component-paid`
8. [ ] - `p2` - Bonus remains bounded; Rating preserves gross value and Billing accounts separately for bonus coverage, platform-borne amount and customer obligation. S-U15 exact evidence/provider/Ledger and detection/enforcement/recovery contracts plus other liability cases still gate launch; no instantaneous cap or unlimited free continuation is promised. Early guarded conversion retains unused credit as above; stacking and payer transfer are refused, and there is no new-period rollover or regrant in v1. A correction does not force a still-active trial to end; valid/applicable same-grant restoration remains usable until original expiry. - `inst-bonus-remains-bounded-rating`

## 3. Processes / Business Logic (CDSL)

### Single-episode arbitration

- [ ] `p1` - **ID**: `cpt-cf-bss-subscriptions-algo-trial-arbitration`

**Steps**:
1. [ ] - `p1` - Early conversion, scheduled conversion and exhaustion conversion (SUB-D-66) use different command keys but share a trial-episode state guard in the committing transaction. Retain exactly one conversion record, predecessor trial terms, successor paid terms and correlation. Duplicate scheduled work after a successful early conversion observes the terminal episode and cannot emit another fact/posture. Cancellation suppressions are read under the same commit boundary. - `inst-early-conversion-scheduled-conversion`
2. [ ] - `p1` - **Initial-trial admission fence.** The no-repeat rule must hold across subscriptions, not only within one episode. Before Billing preparation, resource work or any trial-acquisition capture, at order confirmation (AP08), atomically reserve the authorized payer/plan eligibility key for the stable Orders acquisition origin. A concurrent different acquisition conflicts; an identical origin replays or conflicts on changed content. A live reservation, unknown prior application or retained served-trial history cannot be treated as absent eligibility. Use an indexed unique key and the same scoped local transaction discipline as occupancy; authorization still applies independently. - `inst-initial-trial-admission-fence`
3. [ ] - `p1` - At actual activation, lock/revalidate that admission association and mark it consumed with the episode, commercial state, actual T, receipt and outbox in the same local transaction. Consumed history remains after expiry/cancellation/paid conversion, so a new subscription ID cannot obtain another trial. Pricing initial eligibility must also pass; this local fence does not create a trial offer or replace financial/occupancy protection. - `inst-actual-activation-lock-revalidate`
4. [ ] - `p1` - Release an unapplied reservation only through authoritative non-application settlement fenced against late activation, with any protected payment/claim and prepared-grant obligations resolved by their existing protocols. No timeout or draft cleanup proves non-application. A supported successor attempt retains the same acquisition/admission identity; an already applied episode is recovered, never treated as an unserved retry. Failed acquisition without service does not create served-trial history. This is recovery of initial admission, not permission for a repeat trial or terminal revival. - `inst-release-unapplied-reservation-authoritative`
5. [ ] - `p1` - Exact persistence indexes and SDK transport types are implementation details; the serialized key, consumed-history rule and failure behavior are fixed by this design. - `inst-exact-persistence-indexes-sdk`

### Credit preparation and recovery

- [ ] `p1` - **ID**: `cpt-cf-bss-subscriptions-algo-credit-preparation`

**Steps**:
1. [ ] - `p1` - Use the [trial evidence contract](../DESIGN.md#336-trial-terms-and-provider-evidence) and [initial readiness protocol](05-entitlements.md#initial-activation-readiness-contract). The conditional commitment, its effectiveness at T and Billing's later recording are separate steps. Exact SDK encoding, retained-history budgets and owner conformance remain delivery work. - `inst-use-trial-evidence-contract`

| Evidence | Required meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Identity and association | Durable grant/benefit identity, seller and Orders acquisition/order/version/line, accepted receipt, designated consumer, activation operation and preparation generation. Retain episode/subscription association when established. Transport keys and new attempts cannot issue another grant. |
| Frozen source | PlanRevision, accepted policy and receipt references/digests; amount, currency, payer/beneficiary, eligible products/items/charge kinds and duration semantics. Billing validates authoritative Pricing evidence. No current-catalog substitution, FX or broadened scope. |
| Conditional validity | Reserved grant becomes effective if the exact activation applies at T. Original exclusive expiry follows T and frozen elapsed/calendar policy. Before T no bonus is spendable; delayed recording cannot postpone coverage or move expiry. |
| Lifecycle evidence | Authoritative operation/revision, episode, trial/paid/end boundaries, reason and retained-grant eligibility after early conversion. Effective served time differs from observation/recording time. |
| Recoverable outcome | Commitment/grant revision and digest, command identity/payload digest, pending/refused/applied status, ordered outcome sequence and read barrier. Accounting/allocation and financial completion remain separate provider statuses. Historical remaining balance is not access or paid-service authority. |

2. [ ] - `p1` - Reserve the payer/plan admission key before preparation or trial-acquisition capture. Billing then prepares one durable commitment for the accepted origin and exact activation operation/generation. Freeze the accepted amount, scope and duration without inventing T. Same-content replay returns that commitment; changed content conflicts. A new attempt needs the supported fenced association protocol and retains the same grant. - `inst-reserve-payer-plan-admission`
3. [ ] - `p1` - Persist its authenticated reference/digest in the preparation posture/outbox. Entitlements validates and binds it to the operation, posture, complete manifest and selected profile. Prepare usable access that can continue through T without waiting for Billing's later write. Adapter requests carry assignment scope and desired revision, never subscription ID. Pre-T and never-activated preparation is platform-borne and consumes no bonus. - `inst-persist-authenticated-reference-digest`
4. [ ] - `p1` - At the final local decision, validate accepted terms, exact commitment, current order/financial/security/occupancy guards, deadline and held readiness. Serialize against withdrawal; a provider cannot withdraw coverage between validation and application without an acknowledged consumer fence. Commit actual T, episode/original expiry, coverage reference, state, receipt, posture and recording outbox atomically. No remote call occurs inside that transaction. - `inst-final-local-decision-validate`
5. [ ] - `p1` - Billing consumes or recovers the applied proof and records the same grant effective at T without new issuance/eligibility approval. Verify the exclusive expiry against the frozen policy and Subscriptions boundary; inconsistent evidence refuses. Lost/reordered deliveries recover the original identity. Recording after cancellation or expiry preserves historical effectiveness and current closed eligibility; it cannot revive access or add duration. - `inst-billing-consumes-recovers-applied`
6. [ ] - `p1` - Proved acquisition-level non-application (cancellation or compensation of the purchase) permits settlement of that prepared grant and its trial-admission reservation, with protected claim/payment obligations handled by their own protocol. Closure of one activation attempt or generation does not: a supported successor attempt rebinds the same commitment and reservation to its new operation and generation, and the commitment's exact-operation binding is replaced, not reissued. Unknown or missing activation outcomes retain the commitment and require recovery/escalation. A timeout alone cannot release it. Applied activation takes the historical-recording path, followed by current cancellation/end handling. - `inst-proved-acquisition-level-non`
7. [ ] - `p1` - Preparation and unresolved exposure must remain bounded under the selected service profile: the profile states its limit on outstanding held preparation (count and age per tenant and provider) and the action at that limit, as [the readiness contract](05-entitlements.md#initial-activation-readiness-contract) and gates S-U01/S-U04 require. Save the held-age/count policy/version at authorized preparation start; elapsed held age continues while an Orders pause stops the separate preparation deadline. Orders/Workflow, Subscriptions and Entitlements must agree and test the action that bounds existing held exposure without unauthorized cleanup. The profile stays disabled until those values and the prolonged-outage recovery and release evidence are agreed. Allocation-dependent preparation requires supported same-source continuity and release evidence. Consumable-limit readiness uses provider evidence; readiness-check requests carry a purpose marker, with execution and quota treatment chosen by the provider contract. Failure to support these guarantees disables the affected credit profile before launch. - `inst-preparation-unresolved-exposure-remain`
8. [ ] - `p1` - **Outcome recovery.** Authorized reads by business/grant/operation identity return versioned evidence and eligibility reasons, with allocation and financial-completion statuses. Recover an immutable snapshot/barrier plus ordered subsequent outcomes. Report gaps, retention loss and partial pages explicitly. Not-found, unavailable and unknown are distinct; none proves zero balance, failed issuance, spend permission or customer debt. Unknown or stale outcomes cannot trigger paid conversion. Reconcile every grant outcome against the current episode/paid state before a lifecycle intent. - `inst-outcome-recovery-authorized-reads`
9. [ ] - `p1` - **Served-time classification.** Credit trials retain ordinary tariffs and legitimate gross served facts. Free trials use explicit zero bindings for every covered contribution. From the authoritative trial start, eligible usage uses the same grant even while its Billing record is pending. A valid paid conversion records `T_paid`; each component records `B` and the frozen SUB-D-67 policy version. Non-hourly components use `B = T_paid`. Hourly components use the first UTC-hour boundary at or after `T_paid`, with tariff slices cut at `B`. Status and access change at `T_paid`; service extents are not shifted to `B`. - `inst-served-time-classification-credit`
10. [ ] - `p1` - For an hourly free-period component, `[T_paid, B)` keeps its zero binding. For an hourly credit component, that interval uses the previous tariff, bounded eligible bonus for service strictly before original expiry `E`, and platform coverage for all residual. If credit is exhausted or expired, the platform bears the whole eligible remainder. From `B`, paid-plan terms apply after any valid eligible coverage from the same grant. ## 4. States (CDSL)

### Trial state

- [ ] `p1` - **ID**: `cpt-cf-bss-subscriptions-state-trials`

4. ## 6. Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Trial launch while required S-Q06 authoring/provider/runtime contracts are open refuses before creating service, a hold or a commercial occurrence despite partial direction approval.
- [ ] No applicable authorized settlement route at trial end follows normal expiry/revocation with data retained under existing policy and no automatic successor paid-target boundary/posture/facts, debt or setup charge from expiry alone. Existing served trial facts remain available for rating/bonus allocation. Prior legitimate obligations, paid-retention terms and prepaid-order remedies survive.
- [ ] Definitive prescribed-prepay failure at trial end records failure and closes the episode, while default expiry/revocation retains data and starts no paid period. Conversion retries are permitted only while the episode remains eligible before original E; later payment follows refund or an explicitly assented new paid Orders purchase. No automatic free extension or paid-renewal grace applies; trial extension is deferred, so the current episode ends under its original terms.
- [ ] Prescribed payment confirmed for an eligible episode permits conversion only at a guarded `T_paid` before original E, with non-hourly accrual from `T_paid` and hourly accrual from `B`, with no retroactive debt/coverage for the waiting gap. Expired/cancelled episodes and terminal cancelled subscriptions are not reopened. Payment after closure follows refund or an explicitly assented new paid Orders purchase with its own guarded service start; the applicable supported association route must exist.
- [ ] Unknown payment is recovered as uncertainty, not failure/success/absence. Retry cannot double collect or release protected prepaid rights; it grants neither conversion nor automatic free extension, and payment success does not bypass guards or imply instant Entitlements completion.
- [ ] Accepted invoice/postpay plus authoritative credit-service authorization converts without a linked card or immediate payment, emits ordinary paid-period facts and accrues valid obligations. Invoice/payment follow agreed due terms; no delinquency or dunning is inferred before due/unpaid evidence. No existing/prepaid customer is automatically migrated to credit.
- [ ] Relevant owner unknown/outage is neither established route absence nor authorization and cannot enable conversion or extend trial. An unrelated card-provider outage does not block an otherwise fully evidenced invoice/postpay arrangement.
- [ ] A full Rating replacement corrects eligible pre-paid-boundary trial gross 90 to 110 against grant 100: bonus 100/platform 10/customer 0, even when received after conversion. Preserve gross and original source/tariff/version lineage; duplicate replacement has no additional effect or grant and cannot reopen a closed episode. Residual from component `B` follows accepted paid terms; erroneous customer posting uses the existing idempotent Billing correction/remedy path.
- [ ] Eligible delay-caused trial usage value 5 with bonus 2 allocates 2 to bonus and 3 to platform, without customer debt/negative bonus/top-up. Facts received after conversion retain their actual served-time classification; residual from component `B` follows paid terms, and crossing allocation requires supported evidence without prorating a scalar hourly peak. Erroneous billed trial tail is corrected idempotently.
- [ ] Delayed Entitlements revocation remains observable as pending/completed; expiry alone proves neither instantaneous access removal nor permission to release occupancy before the applicable applied lifecycle transition.
- [ ] Early and scheduled conversion racing under different keys produce one conversion outcome.
- [ ] A cancellation intent, including a transiently failed firing, prevents conversion until explicitly resolved.
- [ ] Suspension parks conversion; terminal cancellation voids it; resume revalidates current accepted terms rather than using expired hold evidence.
- [ ] Conversion/expiry replay after restart preserves the same episode identity and consumer fact history.
- [ ] A crash or lost response between credit-trial activation and Billing finalization recovers the same prepared/grant identity and original T/expiry. Commitment-backed prepared access continues across T despite delayed Billing recording; a newer cancellation/revocation remains authoritative and late recording cannot revive it.

- [ ] Suspend, let the original trial expiry pass, then resume: the episode is expired at its original expiry and stays closed. Resume refuses the parked conversion and retains the normal trial end action. A later paid start requires a separately authorized Orders acquisition, with no backdated period or automatic resource reuse. No trial access, credit or new episode returns.
- [ ] A scheduled prepay conversion starts collection at `E − conversionLeadTime`; a capture still in flight at E, or a degraded Payments, leaves the episode expired at E with access revoked and no debt; a capture that succeeds after E is refunded or, only with explicit customer assent, becomes a new purchase, and never reopens the episode.
- [ ] A postpay-authorized credit trial with `exhaustionAction = convert` exhausts its credit on day 5 of 14: it converts at `T_paid`, with non-hourly paid accrual from `T_paid` and hourly paid accrual from component `B`; stopping-delay and hourly allocation liabilities remain separate from access permission; the same trial with `end`, or without an authorized paid route, is revoked on day 5 with data retained and no debt; a prepay `convert` with payment pending revokes trial access and waits in `conversion-pending` only until original expiry. Success committed before expiry starts paid service once; failure or expiry closes the pending conversion. A late successful collection cannot reopen it and follows the financial remedy/new-purchase route. Restart and duplicate payment signals never collect or convert twice.
- [ ] A free-period trial on a plan with an hourly-peak component converts at 10:37: status, access and non-hourly components are paid from 10:37, the hourly component is rated under the zero binding until 11:00 and paid from 11:00, its term slice is cut at 11:00 and no partial-hour split reaches Rating; the same conversion on a credit trial uses only valid eligible bonus for service before original expiry and assigns the residual to the platform. Exhausted or expired credit is not spent, including expiry between 10:37 and 11:00.
- [ ] Trial extension and repeat requests, including a new subscription for the same payer/plan, refuse before effects; policy permission cannot override deferral. Same-acquisition retries reuse the original episode/grant.

- [ ] Two Orders for the same payer/plan race before preparation/capture: at most one reserves trial admission. Lost replies replay the winner; a new subscription ID cannot bypass consumed history. Fenced unapplied settlement releases only its own reservation; a delayed activation cannot consume a released association.
